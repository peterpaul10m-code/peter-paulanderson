AI Interview Gym — Zara Resume Risk Map V8

Purpose
- Turns confirmed resume evidence into a preparation-priority diagnostic.
- Does not claim access to Micro1/Zara proprietary scoring, hidden prompts, or exact follow-up logic.
- Highlights statements with natural interview follow-up territory so candidates know what they must be able to defend aloud.

Risk signals
- Quantified claim: know the measure, period, source, calculation, and verification.
- Ownership / leadership: separate personal responsibility from team or manager responsibility.
- Outcome / impact: explain attribution and other factors that may have influenced the result.
- Judgment / quality risk: identify the standard, evidence, validation step, or control used.
- Stakeholder complexity: explain disagreement, communication, and confirmation of understanding.
- Technical depth: explain how a listed tool or skill was actually used.
- Broad wording: convert vague claims into concrete, defensible examples.
- Dense claim: practice a one-sentence version before adding detail.

Each Risk Map card includes
1. The exact confirmed resume anchor.
2. Why it creates follow-up territory.
3. Evidence signals detected.
4. A defend checklist covering facts, numbers where applicable, scope, decisions, tradeoffs, and lessons.
5. Follow-up questions to rehearse aloud.

Privacy / integrity
- PDF, DOCX, and TXT resumes are processed in memory by the Resume Lab endpoints.
- Uploaded resume files are not intentionally saved to server storage.
- Candidates review/edit extracted evidence before rebuilding the diagnostic.
- The generator uses confirmed facts and does not invent employers, qualifications, metrics, responsibilities, tools, or outcomes.
- Saving the generated plan is optional and uses browser localStorage on the candidate's device.
