AI Interview Gym · Micro1 / Zara Prep Guide

Speak the reasoning, not the script.

Use this quick guide to structure answers for Micro1/Zara-style automated interviews so your judgment, evidence, tradeoffs, and next step are easy to follow.

1. The five-part delivery route

Direct answerEvidence / exampleReasoningTradeoffNext step

Lead with the conclusion. Add only the strongest evidence. Explain why it supports your decision. Name a meaningful tradeoff when one exists. Close with what you would verify, document, escalate, or monitor next.

2. Train for one clean pass

Do: answer in the first sentence, stay audible while thinking, use one strong example, clarify material ambiguity once, and stop when the answer is complete.

Avoid: long preambles, résumé dumping, silent pauses that look like you are finished, memorized paragraphs, and adding detail after your answer is already complete.

3. Practice the rubric as answer signals

Clarity: Is the answer easy to follow?

Alignment: Did you actually answer the question and follow its constraints?

Evidence: Did you support the decision with a standard, fact, or concrete example?

Judgment: Did you handle ambiguity, tradeoffs, and escalation appropriately?

Delivery: Was the response concise, professional, and complete?

4. Use a 90-second rehearsal clock

For substantive scenario questions, practice delivering a complete answer in about 90 seconds. Short factual questions should be shorter. The goal is signal density, not filling the clock.

5. Final technical check

Test your microphone, camera, browser, and connection. Disable notifications. Keep a browser fallback available. Run at least one full mock so the real interview does not feel like the first time you have spoken under a cumulative clock.
