Before uploading a résumé

Remove information that is not needed for interview preparation, such as full street address, personal identification numbers, banking details, passwords, or unrelated reference contact information. Only upload a résumé you are authorized to use.

Résumé consent control

Resume Lab requires an acknowledgment before the Analyze Resume button becomes available. The server also requires a corresponding privacy-acknowledgment header for résumé-processing requests; requests without it are rejected.

No model training claim

AI Interview Gym does not currently send Resume Lab uploads to an external AI/LLM API. If that architecture changes, the privacy notice and this page should be updated before the change is made available to users.

Browser data

You can remove browser-stored Resume Lab plans, onboarding choices, progress, and attempt history by using the product's clear/reset tools or clearing site data in your browser.
