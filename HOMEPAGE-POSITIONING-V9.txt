AI Interview Gym — Homepage Positioning V9

Purpose
- Keep the business deliberately niche around the Micro1 Generalist opportunity and Zara interview preparation.
- Serve two visitor intents without becoming a generic AI-trainer or generic make-money-online site.

Visitor path 1 — Opportunity discovery
- For people searching for legitimate remote/online work.
- Explain the current Micro1 Generalist opportunity using published Micro1 facts.
- Current snapshot checked September 17, 2026: remote contractor role; advertised pay $50–$90/hour; skills listed include data annotation, critical thinking, attention to detail, written/verbal communication, and remote collaboration.
- Link candidates directly to the live Micro1 job listing.
- State clearly that pay, availability, project scope, hours, eligibility, and hiring decisions can change.
- AI Interview Gym does not employ candidates, submit applications, guarantee work, guarantee income, or guarantee selection.

Visitor path 2 — Existing applicant
- For candidates who have already applied or expect a Zara interview.
- Route directly toward Resume Lab, Resume Risk Map, personalized questions, spoken practice, and mock sessions.
- Position the core principle as: “Prepare what you can defend.”

Homepage structure
1. Micro1-specific hero with two entry CTAs.
2. Two-path intent section.
3. Current Micro1 Generalist opportunity summary with direct live-listing link.
4. Applicant preparation track.
5. Credible explanation of Zara interview pressure without pass guarantees.
6. Resume-personalized preparation method.
7. Narrow Micro1/Zara product proof.
8. Free access gate and freemium preview.

Trust / integrity
- Avoid “available to everybody worldwide.” Use the live listing and caveat geographic/project eligibility.
- Avoid guaranteed pass, guaranteed job, or guaranteed income claims.
- Avoid presenting candidate-reported patterns as official Micro1 rules.
- Do not use JobPosting structured data because AI Interview Gym is not the employer/recruiter.
- Public homepage must not contain protected question-bank or mock-session content.
