Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

AI Interview Gym is an independent interview-preparation product. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Micro1. Paid checkout is currently disabled while commercial infrastructure is being finalized.

What this policy covers

This policy explains how AI Interview Gym handles information when you use the public site, sign up for access, use Resume Lab, save practice progress, or use speech-practice features.

Information you choose to provide

Email: If you submit an email for access or updates, the site may store it in access/lead records needed to provide the requested experience and administer the product.

Résumé: If you use Resume Lab, the file is transmitted to the AI Interview Gym server over HTTPS and processed temporarily in application memory to extract interview-preparation facts. The Resume Lab does not intentionally write the raw résumé to application storage, and the current Resume Lab does not send the résumé to an external AI/LLM API. Extracted facts are returned to your browser so you can review and correct them.

Practice data: Progress, onboarding choices, résumé plans you choose to save, and speech-analytics attempt history may be stored in your browser using local storage or cookies.

Audio and speech recognition

AI Interview Gym does not intentionally upload your recorded practice audio to its own server. Speech analytics are based on a browser-generated transcript. Browser speech-recognition features may be processed locally or by your browser/vendor depending on the browser and device you use; that processing is governed by the browser/vendor's own terms and privacy practices.

Payments

Paid checkout is not currently active. If payment processing is activated, card or payment credentials will be handled by a third-party payment provider rather than stored directly by AI Interview Gym. This policy will be updated before paid sales are enabled.

Technical and security data

The hosting platform and ordinary web infrastructure may process request metadata such as IP address, timestamps, user agent, and security/rate-limit information. AI Interview Gym does not intentionally place résumé contents into application logs.

Retention

The raw Resume Lab file is intended to exist only for the temporary processing needed to analyze the upload. Browser-stored practice information remains on your device until you clear it, reset progress, or clear browser storage. Server-side access/lead records may be retained as needed to operate the service, prevent abuse, and maintain access records.

Sharing

AI Interview Gym does not sell résumé contents or personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers needed to operate the service, such as hosting and—when activated—payment providers.

Your choices

You can skip the optional onboarding email, choose not to upload a résumé, remove sensitive details before upload, edit extracted résumé facts before using them for practice, avoid saving a résumé plan locally, and clear browser-stored progress using the product's reset/clear controls.

Commercial-launch status

A dedicated public privacy/support contact and finalized payment/refund terms will be published before paid sales are enabled. This policy describes the current beta product behavior and will be updated when those systems change.
